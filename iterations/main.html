Imagine that the array is a box of apples that was brought from a warehouse. Iteration is the process of taking each apple out of the box one at a time to do something with it: see if it's rotten; wash it; weigh; or transfer it to another box, but without the worms.

In web development, the “apples box” is data. List of products in the cart, posts in the Instagram feed, users in the admin panel. Iteration is needed to turn raw data into useful results (for example, drawing a list on the screen or calculating the total amount of an order).
